Illustrative only — not a quote. Figures compare Stripe’s published Australian card pricing with an assumed flat Tenvo fee the joint venture would set. They exclude GST unless toggled, assume payments are currently taken by card through ClubSpark, and ignore failed-payment, refund and onboarding handling. Savings accrue to clubs (or, until 1 October 2026, to members where clubs currently surcharge).
